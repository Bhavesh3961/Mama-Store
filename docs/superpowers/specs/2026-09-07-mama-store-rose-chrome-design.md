# MAMA STORE Rose Chrome Design

## Goal

Rebuild the MAMA STORE storefront as a distinctive, conversion-focused cosmetic shop with a rose-chrome glass visual language, not a generic effect showcase.

## Visual system

- Use a deep plum-to-rose background, warm pearl surfaces, restrained blur, and metallic highlights.
- Replace the text-only wordmark with a compact custom MAMA monogram and editorial wordmark built as local SVG.
- Keep 3D as one hero product sculpture and small, motion-safe product accents. Remove competing 3D stages, scratch cards, and decorative widgets from the primary path.
- Use real product imagery already supplied by catalog data. Never use placeholder stock-art panels.

## Homepage

- The hero shows the custom logo, a concise MAMA STORE value proposition, product discovery and WhatsApp actions, and a floating 3D-inspired cosmetic sculpture.
- A compact trust strip follows the hero: authentic products, Pali delivery, and a free-gift threshold.
- Product discovery prioritizes categories, bestselling products, and current offers.
- The existing cart and checkout flow remains available. WhatsApp stays as an equal, practical order route.

## Quality requirements

- Preserve existing routes and product data contracts.
- Keep all interactions keyboard accessible and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid inflated marketing copy, emoji UI, excessive gradients, and duplicate calls to action.
- Build, type-check, and inspect the final homepage at desktop and mobile widths.
